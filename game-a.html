Letter Recognition

Letter bubbles float up the screen. Press the matching key on your keyboard (or tap the bubble) to pop it before it escapes through the top!

Choose difficulty

  • Correct key pops the bubble: +10 points.
  • Wrong key (no such bubble on screen) costs a penalty points and flashes red.
  • Miss = a bubble touches the red line at the top. Too many misses and it's game over.
  • Level up every 5 pops: bubbles get faster and more of them appear.
